# Sharing your website data with us

To see which pages and content actually work on your site, we'd like your Google Analytics and
Google Search Console numbers. Two options; **option A is quicker for you and gives us more
complete data.**

The data is aggregate: page-level visits, engagement and search performance, never information
about individual people. We only read it, and we keep it with your project files.

---

## Option A: give us read-only access (about 2 minutes)

**Google Analytics 4**
1. Open Google Analytics → **Admin** (gear icon, bottom left).
2. Under *Property*, open **Property access management**.
3. Click **+** → **Add users**, enter our email address, choose the **Viewer** role, and save.

**Google Search Console**
1. Open Search Console and select your site.
2. Go to **Settings** → **Users and permissions** → **Add user**.
3. Enter our email address, choose **Restricted** permission, and save.

Then send us your GA4 **property ID**: Admin → Property settings, a number such as `123456789`
(not the code that starts with `G-`).

---

## Option B: send us exports (about 10 minutes)

Please use the **last 12 months**, or the period since your last redesign if that's shorter.
Send the files as they download: no need to open, edit or combine them.

**1. Google Analytics 4: landing pages**
1. **Reports** → **Engagement** → **Landing page**.
2. Set the date range (top right).
3. Optional but helpful: click the **+** next to the *Landing page* column header and add **Session default channel group**.
4. Click the **share** icon (top right) → **Download file** → **Download CSV**.

**2. Google Analytics 4: pages**
1. **Reports** → **Engagement** → **Pages and screens**.
2. Make sure the first column shows **Page path and screen class** (use the dropdown above the column to switch).
3. Same date range, then **share** icon → **Download file** → **Download CSV**.

**3. Google Search Console: search performance**
1. **Performance** → **Search results**.
2. Click the date filter and choose **Last 12 months** (or **Last 16 months**, the maximum).
3. Click **Export** (top right) → **Download CSV**. You'll get a zip file; send it as it is.

Google Sheets or Excel versions of the same reports work too.

Note: Search Console's export includes at most 1,000 pages and 1,000 search queries. For larger
sites, option A gives us the full picture.

Thank you!
